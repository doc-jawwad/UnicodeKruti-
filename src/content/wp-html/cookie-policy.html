1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your computer, smartphone, or other device when you visit a website.

They help websites:

  • Remember your preferences
  • Improve website performance
  • Understand how visitors use the website
  • Enhance user experience
  • Support website security
  • Deliver relevant advertisements (where applicable)

Cookies generally do not contain information that directly identifies you personally.
