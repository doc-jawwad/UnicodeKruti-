Welcome to UnicodeKruti ("Website," "we," "our," or "us"). Your privacy is important to us, and we are committed to protecting your personal information while providing a secure and reliable online conversion tool.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your personal data when you use our website.

1. Who We Are

UnicodeKruti is an online tool that allows users to convert Hindi text between Unicode and KrutiDev font formats quickly and securely.

Our goal is to provide a fast, accurate, privacy-focused conversion experience without requiring users to create an account or upload documents.

2. Text Conversion Privacy

No text is stored or transmitted during conversion. Conversion runs entirely in your browser on your device. The Hindi text you paste into the converter is never uploaded to our servers, never written to a database, and never sent over the network as part of the conversion process.

The text you paste into our converter is not stored on our servers, not permanently recorded, and not used to train artificial intelligence models.

We do not read, sell, or analyze the content of the text you convert.

Optional local features such as “recent conversions” history, if enabled in your browser, stay in your browser’s local storage on your device only. Clearing site data removes them. That history is never sent to UnicodeKruti.

For confidential government documents, office files, legal drafts, educational material, or personal notes, we recommend verifying that you are using the official website over a secure HTTPS connection before using the converter.

4. Cookies

UnicodeKruti does not use cookies for advertising or for selling your data. Cookies and similar technologies that may be set are limited to the following:

  • Microsoft Clarity — session and analytics cookies used for heatmaps and session insights (named tool: Microsoft Clarity).
  • Google Analytics (GA4) — analytics cookies when Google Analytics is enabled on the site (for aggregated traffic and page-view measurement).
  • Essential / functional storage — browser local storage for tool preferences and recent-conversion history on your device only (not a tracking cookie).

We do not use cookies solely to track you across unrelated third-party sites for advertising. You can disable analytics cookies through your browser settings; the converter itself continues to work without them.

Cookies do not generally identify you by name. See also our Cookie Policy for more detail.

5. Analytics

We use Microsoft Clarity (a Microsoft analytics product) to understand how visitors use the site — for example which pages are viewed and where the interface is hard to use. Clarity may record anonymized session data and set cookies as described in Section 4.

When configured, we also use Google Analytics (GA4) for aggregated traffic statistics. We may use Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools for search indexing diagnostics; those tools run on our accounts and do not place advertising cookies on your device through this website.

Analytics data is aggregated where possible and is not used to identify you by name. Converted Hindi text is never sent to Clarity, Google Analytics, or any other analytics service.
