Welcome to KrutiDev ↔ Unicode Converter, a dedicated platform built to make Hindi font conversion simple, accurate, and accessible for everyone.

Our website focuses exclusively on converting text between Unicode and KrutiDev, one of the most widely used legacy Hindi font systems. Whether you're a student, government employee, typist, translator, publisher, educator, or office professional, our goal is to help you convert Hindi text quickly and reliably without unnecessary complexity.

Our Mission

Hindi typing and document conversion remain challenging because many government offices, educational institutions, and organizations continue to use legacy fonts such as KrutiDev, while modern computers, websites, and applications primarily use Unicode.

Our mission is to bridge that gap by providing:

  • Fast Unicode ↔ KrutiDev conversion
  • Accurate character mapping
  • Easy-to-use online tools
  • Educational guides and tutorials
  • Reliable resources for Hindi typing and font conversion

We believe technology should make working with Hindi documents easier, not more complicated.

Why We Built This Website
